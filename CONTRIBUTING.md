# Contributing to [littel-pwl-support]

Thank you for your interest in contributing! Here are the guidelines to help you get started.

---

## 1. Reporting Bugs & Requesting Features
* Check the **Issues** tab first to see if your bug or request has already been reported.
* If not, open a new issue with a clear title and description.
* For bugs, please include:
  * Steps to reproduce the issue
  * Expected vs. actual behavior
  * Your operating system and environment/runtime version

---
